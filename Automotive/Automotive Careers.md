# Automotive Industry Career Portals

A curated collection of official automotive company career portals for students, fresh graduates, embedded engineers, firmware engineers, automotive software developers, validation engineers, testing engineers, and experienced professionals.

## How to Use

1. Identify companies matching your skills and interests.
2. Visit the official career portal.
3. Search for roles such as:

   * Embedded Engineer
   * Firmware Engineer
   * AUTOSAR Engineer
   * Functional Safety Engineer
   * Automotive Software Engineer
   * Validation Engineer
   * ECU Development Engineer
   * HIL Testing Engineer
   * System Engineer
   * Vehicle Integration Engineer
