# Embedded Careers Hub

A professionally curated collection of official company career portals focused on:

* Embedded Systems
* Real-Time Systems
* Firmware Engineering
* Semiconductor & VLSI
* Internet of Things (IoT)
* Robotics
* Aerospace & Defense
* Telecommunications
* Industrial Automation
* AI & Edge Computing

This repository helps students, fresh graduates, researchers, and experienced professionals directly access official company career portals without relying solely on third-party job boards.

---

# Purpose

Finding legitimate career opportunities across embedded and deep-tech industries can be time-consuming.

This repository consolidates official company career portals into domain-specific directories, enabling faster job searches and direct applications.

The focus is on:

* Official company career websites
* Engineering-focused organizations
* Product companies
* Research organizations
* Semiconductor companies
* Aerospace and defense companies
* Robotics and automation companies
* AI and Edge Computing companies
